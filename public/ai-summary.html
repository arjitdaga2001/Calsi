About CALSI.IN

CALSI.IN is India's leading platform for free, expert-verified financial calculators. It is designed for Indian retail investors, salaried professionals, and students to plan their finances smartly. All tools are verified against RBI, SEBI, AMFI, and Income Tax Department data.

Our Tools

  • SIP Calculator: Calculate returns on Systematic Investment Plans.
  • EMI Calculator: Plan your home, car, and personal loan EMIs.
  • Income Tax Calculator: Compare Old vs New Tax Regimes (AY 2026-27).
  • PPF Calculator: Calculate Public Provident Fund maturity amount.
  • FD Calculator: Calculate Fixed Deposit interest.
  • GST Calculator: Add or remove GST from prices.
  • NPS Calculator: Plan your National Pension System corpus.
  • Lumpsum Calculator: Calculate mutual fund lumpsum returns.
  • RD Calculator: Recurring Deposit return calculation.
  • SWP Calculator: Systematic Withdrawal Plan calculator.

Trust & Verification

All data is sourced from official sources like RBI, SEBI, AMFI, and the Income Tax Department of India. Data is current for FY 2025-26 / AY 2026-27.
