About CALSI.IN

CALSI.IN is India's leading platform for free, expert-verified financial calculators. It is designed for Indian retail investors, salaried professionals, and students to plan their finances smartly. All tools are verified against official directives from RBI, SEBI, AMFI, and the Income Tax Department of India.

E-E-A-T (Experience, Expertise, Authoritativeness, and Trustworthiness)

  • Expertise: Calculators are built using standard actuarial and financial formulas used by leading Indian banks and institutions.
  • Authority: Tax and financial parameters are strictly updated for the current Financial Year (FY 2025-26 / AY 2026-27).
  • Trustworthiness: 100% free, no login required, privacy-first (no personal financial data is stored on our servers).
